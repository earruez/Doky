# Vehifolio Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 3, 2026**

Vehifolio is designed to keep vehicle records private and under the user's control. The app does not require a Vehifolio account and does not operate a server that stores a user's vehicle history.

## Data stored on the device

Vehicle details, odometer readings, fuel entries, maintenance records, reminders, notes, documents, scans, photos, backups, and calculated ownership insights are stored locally on the user's device by default.

Vehifolio does not sell personal data and does not use vehicle history for advertising or cross-app tracking.

## iCloud and Shared Garage

iCloud features are optional. If a user enables them, Vehifolio uses Apple's CloudKit to place selected vehicle data in the user's private iCloud storage or in a CloudKit share the user explicitly creates or accepts. Apple provides and operates that infrastructure under the user's Apple account. Vehifolio does not copy those vehicle records to a Vehifolio-operated backend.

Users control whether iCloud features are enabled and who is invited to a shared garage.

## Purchases

Apple processes App Store payments. Vehifolio uses RevenueCat to determine whether a Vehifolio Pro entitlement is active and to support purchase restoration. RevenueCat may process App Store transaction and app-install identifiers that are necessary to provide purchase status. Vehifolio does not receive the user's full payment-card details.

## Camera, photos, and documents

Camera and photo-library access is used only when the user chooses to capture or attach vehicle information. Document images that Vehifolio stores are re-encoded to remove embedded image metadata such as EXIF location information when supported by the file format.

The user can delete stored vehicle documents from inside Vehifolio.

## Notifications

Vehifolio can schedule local reminders for vehicle maintenance and document expiry. Notification permission is optional and can be changed in iOS Settings.

## App Lock

If App Lock is enabled, Vehifolio asks iOS to authenticate with Face ID, Touch ID, or the device passcode. Vehifolio does not receive or store biometric templates or the device passcode.

## Backups and exports

Backups and exports are created only when requested by the user. After an export leaves Vehifolio, the user controls where it is saved or shared and which third-party service, if any, receives it.

## Data deletion

Because the vehicle database is local-first, deleting records in the app or deleting the app removes the corresponding local Vehifolio data, subject to any backups the user has separately retained. Data synchronized through iCloud is also subject to Apple's iCloud and CloudKit controls and any active shares.

## Children

Vehifolio is a vehicle record-management utility and is not directed to children.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Vehifolio adds or changes features. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

## Contact

Privacy or support questions can be sent through the support contact published with Vehifolio's App Store listing.
